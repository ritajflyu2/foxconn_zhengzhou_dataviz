# Foxconn Zhengzhou environmental data — findings memo

Generated by `scripts/environment_data.py` from the existing 环境/用工/自动化 workbooks. No
source file was modified; all output lives in this folder (`tables/`, `charts/`).
Re-run with `python3 scripts/environment_data.py` from the repo root to regenerate everything.

Evidence strength key: **strong** = official/administrative record, or a figure
corroborated across independent sources / cross-checked by the script itself;
**medium** = a single credible self-reported source with plausible but unverified
figures; **weak** = a modeled/derived estimate, small sample, mismatched scopes,
or figures that conflict across sources.

---

## D1 — Monthly emissions, 2020–2025

**Medium** (self-reported monthly executive reports; internally consistent).

- Six charts cover this question, all sharing one underlying tidy table
  (`tables/D1_monthly_emissions_wide.csv`, restricted to the "全厂合计" scope so
  sub-categories like 一般/主要排放口 aren't double-counted):
  - `charts/D1a_monthly_emissions_small_multiples.png` — both plants, all four
    pollutants, monthly trend.
  - `charts/D1b_seasonality_cod_by_calendar_month.png` — both plants, COD only,
    by calendar month.
  - `charts/D1c_hongfujin_only_monthly_emissions.png` /
    `charts/D1d_hongfujin_only_seasonality_by_calendar_month.png` — **鸿富锦
    (Hongfujin) only**, all four pollutants (no 富联精密 data at all).
  - `charts/D1e_fii_precision_only_monthly_emissions.png` /
    `charts/D1f_fii_precision_only_seasonality_by_calendar_month.png` — **富联精密
    (FII Precision) only**, all four pollutants (no Hongfujin data at all).
  The single-plant pairs (D1c/D1d, D1e/D1f) were added per review feedback
  asking to see within-plant variation without comparing the two plants against
  each other. FII Precision keeps its orange color from D1a/D1b/D2b throughout,
  for visual consistency across the whole analysis.
- **富联精密 has data only from 2020-09** (its permit/executive reporting starts
  later than 鸿富锦's) — Jan-Aug 2020 report as exactly 0 for **all four**
  pollutants in the source file, which is a no-data stretch, not real zero
  emissions (see D1e). Its VOCs are additionally reported as exactly 0 through
  2020–2022 even after reporting starts, and only become non-zero from 2023
  (19.67 t) — flagged `VOCs=0_likely_not_reported` rather than treated as a true
  zero. Neither issue is visible in the Hongfujin-only charts.
- **Outlier flag (automated, not just called out by the brief):** the script's own
  "> 3× the 7-month rolling median" rule independently re-derives the same
  2024-09 Hongfujin COD outlier named in the research brief (152.6 t vs. a 39.5 t
  rolling median).
- **Within Hongfujin, the four pollutants move independently** (D1c): COD and
  NH3-N both step up sharply from December 2021 into January 2022 and stay
  elevated through the rest of the series, while VOCs move the opposite way —
  dropping sharply over the same transition and trending down thereafter.
  Particulates report as exactly 0 in every month on record for this plant.
- **Within FII Precision, particulates are the more active series** (D1e) —
  unlike Hongfujin's flat-zero particulates, FII Precision's particulates move
  substantially month to month (roughly 0.3–2.2 t), while its COD/NH3-N/VOCs
  each have their own distinct step patterns tied to specific dates rather than
  a shared trend.
- **Seasonality:** boxplots by calendar month across all years do **not** show a
  clean, systematic Aug–Oct peak for either plant or any of the four pollutants.
  For Hongfujin (D1b, D1d), Aug–Oct medians are close to the other months' once
  the 2024-09 outlier is set aside. For FII Precision (D1f), COD's October
  median is its year's single highest point, but July and December run nearly
  as high, so this reads as a second-half-of-year pattern rather than an
  Aug-Oct-specific one, and its comparison is additionally confounded by the
  Jan-Aug-2020 no-data stretch depressing only the first-half-of-year months in
  year one; particulates there actually peak in May, not Aug-Oct. This is a
  **negative finding** across both plants: the data does not clearly support
  "emissions peak with the iPhone production ramp"; monthly swings look more
  plausibly tied to discrete events (technical upgrades, treatment-facility
  commissioning) than to a calendar-driven production cycle.

## D2 — Actual vs. approved (EIA design) emissions

**Medium-strong** for 鸿富锦 (a dedicated, internally-reconciled sheet); **weak**
for 富联精密 (no single comparable scope exists across its EIA filings).

- 鸿富锦's cumulative EIA-approved VOCs/NMHC (600.18 t/a since 2017) sits far above
  actual reported VOCs (22–81 t/a, i.e. **4–14% of approved** — approvals carried
  large headroom). COD runs the opposite direction: approved cumulative COD
  (161.72 t/a) is **exceeded roughly 3×** by actual 2024–2025 COD (446–485 t/a).
  See `charts/D2a_hongfujin_approved_vs_actual.png` and
  `tables/D2_hongfujin_approved_vs_actual.csv`.
- **Important caveat, stated directly in the chart**: approved COD is mostly a
  single production-wastewater figure per project; actual COD is a self-reported
  whole-plant total that may include domestic sewage. The scopes likely differ,
  so this is presented as "two numbers, compared" — **not** as an exceedance
  finding.
- 富联精密/富泰华 has no equivalent single cumulative-approved number — each EIA
  filing re-states COD under a different scope (生产废水 only, or "生产+生活，厂界"
  combined). The one directly comparable year (2022, combined scope = 146.64 t/a)
  shows actual reported COD at only 59.56 t/a (~41%) — the **opposite** pattern
  from 鸿富锦. Kept as a weak, single-year comparison
  (`tables/D2_fulianjingmi_eia_cod_by_scope.csv`).

## D3 — Emissions relative to workers and output

**Medium** for the underlying series (official insured-worker counts + self-reported
annual totals); the "intensity" framing itself is explicitly **not** a causal claim.

- Indexed to 2020=100 on a single shared axis (no dual-axis chart), both plants
  show insured headcount falling to ~50–90% of its 2020 level while COD emitted
  rises to 1.1–3.2× its 2020 level — `charts/D3a_workers_vs_cod_indexed.png`.
- Per-insured-worker COD intensity is presented separately
  (`charts/D3b_cod_intensity_per_worker.png`): 鸿富锦 rises from ~2.3 kg/person/yr
  (2020) to ~14.5 kg/person/yr (2025). As the research brief anticipated, this is
  flagged explicitly as a **mechanical** effect of a shrinking denominator, framed
  as "fewer workers, emissions did not fall" rather than any behavioral claim.
- Design-headcount denominator is only usable for 富联精密/富泰华 (constant 23,000
  across all 2013–2022 whole-plant EIA filings) — 鸿富锦 has no comparable
  whole-plant design figure post-2017 (see the A1 labor redesign), so that column
  is left blank for 鸿富锦 rather than approximated.

## D4 — Cumulative approved emissions as the site expanded, 2010–2026

**Strong** (a clean, fully-attributable derivation from named EIA project filings).

- Each of 鸿富锦's seven assembly-project EIAs (K→F→C/L→D→B/E→G, 2010–2017) is
  summed in approval order; the cumulative total flattens after 2017 (no further
  assembly-project EIA exists) at NMHC 600.18 t/a / COD 158.34–161.72 t/a
  (small residual gap vs. D2's number traced to a later filing's minor revision,
  noted in the table). See `charts/D4_hongfujin_cumulative_approved_by_zone.png`.
- This is explicitly an **approved-design** build-up, not measured emissions, and
  says nothing about whether all seven zones still operate at their original
  designed scale today.

## D5 — Where labor and environment connect directly

**Strong** — the clearest, most directly-sourced finding in this analysis.

- 富联裕展's domestic-sewage COD and design headcount move together because the
  EIA computes the former **from** the latter (a per-person sewage coefficient):
  66.7 t/a (headcount 26,000, 2022) → 39.1 t/a (headcount 11,614, 2023) → 43.2 t/a
  (headcount held at 11,614 through 2025, the most recent whole-plant filing).
  The 2023 EIA text itself states "生活污水COD下降与定员26000→11614人同步." See
  `charts/D5_domestic_cod_vs_design_headcount.png` — the one chart in this project
  that uses a dual axis, because both quantities come from the same document's
  stated arithmetic relationship (not an external pairing of unrelated series).
- Scope is narrow: this link holds **only** for domestic sewage. Production
  wastewater COD, VOCs, etc. are driven by process/capacity (see D3), not by
  headcount, and should not be read through the same lens.

## D6 — Group-level carbon footprint (background only, not Zhengzhou)

**Medium** (third-party ESG data provider; explicitly a different, consolidated
reporting boundary from anything else in this analysis).

- Hon Hai's and Foxconn Industrial Internet's Scope 1/2/Scope 3-upstream emissions
  and revenue-normalized intensity are tidied and charted
  (`charts/D6a_trucost_scope_emissions_by_company.png`,
  `charts/D6b_trucost_intensity_per_revenue.png`). Scope 3 upstream (supply chain)
  is 1–2 orders of magnitude larger than Scope 1 (own operations) for both
  companies — the group's disclosed footprint is overwhelmingly a supply-chain
  story, not a factory-floor one.
- Scope 1 shows large level shifts in 2017–2019 and 2022–2025 that most likely
  reflect a change in disclosure method (CDP-reported vs. modeled estimate; see
  the `GHG加权披露率` field) rather than an actual operational step-change.
- **Used only as context** — this is consolidated group data and cannot be
  disaggregated to Zhengzhou; no chart in this section should be read as a
  Zhengzhou-specific number.

## Known cross-cutting limitations

1. **EIA figures are design/approved values, not measurements**; permit execution
   reports are self-reported and not independently audited in this dataset.
2. **Scopes differ across EIA filings** (single project vs. whole plant;
   production wastewater vs. domestic sewage) — every chart that compares an EIA
   number to anything else says so, and the underlying 口径/数据性质 columns are
   kept in the tables for anyone who needs to re-filter.
3. **Permit execution reports cover only 2020 onward and only two of the four
   Zhengzhou entities** (鸿富锦, 富联精密). 富联裕展 and 河南富驰 have no comparable
   monthly panel; D1–D3's monthly/annual analysis is limited to the two plants
   the source file covers.
4. **Units**: tonnes/year unless noted; wastewater volume in 万m³/a in EIA tables.
5. One data-quality contradiction (D7, 富联裕展's 2025 all-zero report) and one
   unresolved EIA internal inconsistency (D1's 富联裕展 18,000-vs-20,400 headcount
   note, carried over from the A1 labor redesign) are flagged but **not**
   resolved — flagged explicitly rather than silently picked one way.
