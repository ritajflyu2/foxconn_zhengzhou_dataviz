# Foxconn Zhengzhou automation data — findings memo

Generated by `scripts/automation_data.py` from data/automation/自动化_环评设备定员产能面板与月度时间线_2010-2026.xlsx
(main data source) and data/automation/自动化_环评文件检索记录_2010-2026.md (the EIA document
retrieval log — used for the Hongfujin 2010–2017 capacity figures in E6, and as
the source-document trail behind the E4 quotes). No source file was modified;
all output lives in this folder (`tables/`, `charts/`). Re-run with
`python3 scripts/automation_data.py` from the repo root to regenerate everything.

Evidence strength key: **strong** = official/administrative record, or a figure
corroborated across independent sources / cross-checked by the script itself;
**medium** = a single credible self-reported source with plausible but
unverified figures; **weak** = a modeled/derived estimate, small sample,
mismatched scopes, or figures that conflict across sources.

**Cross-cutting story this section tests**: did Foxconn Zhengzhou produce the
same or more with fewer (insured, designed) workers and more machines? Every
finding below is qualified by what it can and can't say about that question —
none of them is a measurement of actual employment, actual output, or actual
automation-caused job loss; all of them stay at the level of what EIA/company
documents themselves state (design headcount, design capacity, stated reasons,
listed equipment).

## E1 — Headcount vs. machines over time (FII Yuzhan, A/C/D/E zones)

**Medium** (EIA-stated design figures, internally consistent across four filings).

- FII Yuzhan's design headcount for these zones fell in four steps: 51,000
  (2020-12) → 30,000 (2021-09) → 26,000 (2022-05) → 11,614 (2023-05). CNC count
  stayed in a ~4,500–5,300 band across the same period (2021 has no equipment
  table on file). See `charts/E1_yuzhan_headcount_vs_cnc.png`.
- **Decomposed per the review feedback**: CNC-per-worker and robots-per-1,000-
  workers both rise sharply (0.10 → 0.46 CNC/worker), but the chart shows all
  three series side by side specifically so this isn't misread as "more
  automation" — the rise is driven almost entirely by the shrinking
  denominator (design headcount falling), not by more machines being
  installed. The two underlying series are also in `tables/E1_yuzhan_headcount_vs_machines.csv`
  for anyone who wants to recompute the ratio differently.

## E2 — Output with a fixed workforce (FII Precision / Futaihua)

**Medium** (EIA-stated design figures across four filings, 2017–2022).

- Every filing for this plant states the same "23,000 people, no new hires"
  design-headcount commitment while design capacity rises from 70 to ~110
  thousand units/day. CNC count over the same years (6,272 → 4,540 → 4,531)
  does not grow with capacity either. See `charts/E2_futaihua_capacity_vs_headcount.png`.
- **Labeled strictly as design figures, per the review feedback** — the
  headcount-per-1,000-units-of-capacity panel is captioned as a design-basis
  ratio, never as "productivity" or "output per worker," since none of this is
  a measured actual-output number.

## E3 — What machines were added?

**Medium** (a keyword-classified 915-row equipment list; the classification
itself is spot-checked, not independently audited).

- Before/after equipment counts by category (CNC, robots/robotic arms,
  automation-specific machines, inspection equipment, manual stations, other)
  for all nine projects with an equipment table, faceted in
  `charts/E3_equipment_before_after_by_project.png`.
- **Spot-check run before trusting the robot count as fact** (per the review's
  explicit ask): 3 of 915 raw device rows name both a CNC and a 机械手/robotic-
  arm function in one line item, so CNC and robot totals overlap slightly
  rather than being a clean single-function count — flagged in the chart note,
  not glossed over.
- **Planned vs. actually-built kept as separate series, never summed**: only
  rows marked 已建成 ("built") or the 2017 pre-retrofit baseline are treated as
  confirmed; every other 状态说明 wording (e.g. "计划数，尚未建成") is planned/
  unconfirmed and shown faded in the chart. 6 of 9 projects are majority
  planned/unconfirmed by this rule — see `tables/E3_project_status_majority.csv`.

## E4 — What reasons do companies give for reducing headcount?

**Strong** as a documented-statement exercise (every quote is verbatim from the
filing, with its own date and source cited) — explicitly **not** a claim about
the true cause of any headcount change.

- Five quotes collected from the project panel's own 定员口径/备注 fields, each
  carrying its date, entity, and source cell
  (`tables/E4_headcount_change_reasons_quotes.csv`).
- **Automation and capacity reduction kept as separate categories, per the
  review feedback**: 4 filings attribute a headcount change (or its absence
  despite added capacity) to an automation upgrade; 1 filing (FII Yuzhan,
  2022-05, 30,000 → 26,000) attributes its reduction explicitly to "capacity
  reduction," not automation. `charts/E4_headcount_reduction_reasons.png`
  colors FII Yuzhan's four-step headcount decline by each step's own stated
  reason, so the one non-automation step is visually distinct rather than
  blended into an "automation caused all of this" narrative.
- Hongfujin's 2010–2017 project history (in the EIA retrieval log's section B)
  contributes no quotes here — none of those seven filings use
  automation/capacity-reduction language; they are new-capacity approvals, not
  reductions (see E6).

## E5 — Timing: automation events vs. labor and emissions

**Weak-medium**, explicitly illustrative only, per the review's ask to scale
back ambition.

- Two single-entity overlays, never pooled across legal entities:
  - `charts/E5a_futaihua_timing_overlay.png` — FII Precision/Futaihua design
    headcount, site-wide recruitment rebate, and **this same entity's own**
    monthly COD (legitimate pairing: Futaihua was renamed FII Precision, so
    the design-headcount and emissions series share one legal-entity history).
  - `charts/E5b_yuzhan_timing_overlay.png` — FII Yuzhan design headcount and
    site-wide hourly-worker rate. No FII-Yuzhan-specific emissions series
    exists in this dataset (the monthly emissions panel covers Hongfujin and
    FII Precision, a different legal-entity chain), so no emissions panel is
    force-paired here rather than mislabeling a different entity's emissions
    as this one's.
- Both charts are presented as "what happened side by side in time," with no
  summary "automation correlates with X" claim — there are few events and many
  confounders (iPhone product cycles, COVID, tariffs, self-reported monthly
  data) across the whole timeline, which the research question's own feedback
  flagged as unsupportable for a cross-entity claim.

## E6 — Baseline: labor intensity of the early assembly era (Hongfujin, 2010–2017)

**Medium** (design headcount from the main workbook; design capacity only
available from the EIA document retrieval log, both approved planning figures,
not measurements).

- Design headcount per 10,000 units/year of design capacity, by zone, before
  any automation-upgrade language appears in these filings — ranges from 9.8
  (Zone C) to 37.5 (Zone G) people per 10k units/yr, a large spread even within
  this "no automation yet" era. See `charts/E6_hongfujin_baseline_labor_intensity.png`
  and `tables/E6_hongfujin_baseline_labor_intensity.csv`.
- **Zone D's capacity figure is flagged, not corrected**: the source document
  contains an apparent typo ("10900万万件"), and the resulting ratio (2.8) is
  shown in grey with an explicit "unreliable" flag rather than silently
  dropped or guessed at.
- This baseline exists to give E1–E4's automation-era ratios something to
  compare against — it is purely descriptive on its own.

## Known cross-cutting limitations

1. **Every "design" figure in this analysis (headcount, capacity) is an EIA
   planning value**, not measured actual employment or output. Scopes differ
   between filings (single project vs. whole plant) — filter by the source
   workbook's own 定员口径 column before combining any two rows.
2. **Equipment "after" counts are sometimes superseded plans**, not confirmed
   installations (see E3). Three of the source workbook's own EIA filings were
   scanned documents read by OCR; their headcount/capacity figures were
   cross-checked against later filings, but their equipment tables were not
   used (OCR quality too poor).
3. **Construction periods are planned dates**; actual timing uses
   commissioning/acceptance dates where available. Hongfujin's 2010–2017
   projects have year-level dates only, no month.
4. **Every per-worker/per-headcount ratio in this section uses a DESIGN figure
   in the denominator** — design headcount can fall for reasons that have
   nothing to do with automation (see E1's and E4's findings above, and the
   labor analysis's dispatch-worker findings). A rising ratio is never, by
   itself, evidence of "more automation" — every chart using one here also
   shows the underlying headcount and equipment/output series separately.
5. **Hongfujin (整机组装, whole-handset assembly, unit = 10k handsets) and FII
   Precision/FII Yuzhan (金属机构件, metal structural parts, unit = 1,000
   pieces/day) are not directly comparable** — each is only used for its own
   time series, never combined into one cross-plant capacity figure.
